# 2026-11-21

## D-034 reshaped

Dropped the `--sil` mode. A `keelson` bus kind instead, with self-sufficiency as
a property of the bus kind and a manifest-level `self_sufficient` the compiler
checks against the buses. Release builds refuse `false`. That trades the
structural "sim data never reaches a real actuator" for mixed bench setups,
which are wanted. No zenoh access control: security is assumed at the network
boundary, same as 0183-over-UDP, where we never designed it either.

Missed on the first pass and caught on re-read: effectors on a `keelson` bus
put actuator commands on zenoh pub/sub, which is what D-011 forbids, with no
D-027 failsafe path. Written in as a test-build exception rather than left
implicit; self-sufficient manifests keep both rules intact.

## Vocabulary, and Keelson 0.6 command authority

Planning the keelson bus exposed loose words: "backend" meant both output
backends and `--sim` versus buses, "Keelson" meant the adapter, the wire
format, and now a bus kind, and claimant and observer were one interface on
both sides. Fixed the words in docs/glossary.md and proposed D-035 to separate
claimants from telemetry receivers.

Surprise from the Keelson target bump to 0.6.0-pre.19: Keelson now has its own
`command_authority` lease with holder, claimant, and observer roles, no arbiter,
and no seizure. Same words as ours, different semantics. It composes rather than
conflicts: the lease is between stations above, and Keelson already names the
onboard veto `operational_authority`, which is what the supervisor is. Order
agreed: words and D-035 on paper, then the Keelson migration, then the bus.
