# 2026-11-26

## D-035 rewritten after keelson #399

Keelson #399 proposes `conn/v1`, adopted from Coxswain's conn protocol. The
first reading was to co-own it. Rejected: a jointly owned wire format ties
Coxswain's pace to Keelson's, and Coxswain has to run with Keelson absent
anyway. D-035 now says Coxswain owns the conn's meaning (the supervisor's rules
and the contract types) and the Keelson adapter implements whatever standard
interfaces Keelson settles on, translating. Share vocabulary, own contracts.

A first draft refused `vehicle_navigation/v1` and `vehicle_lifecycle/v1`
arm/disarm as "needing the adapter to act as a claimant". Wrong on the facts:
`Setpoint` already has `StationKeep` and `FollowPath`, the supervisor already
has holder-only `arm`/`disarm`, and liveness is the claimant's heartbeat, not a
setpoint stream. So both are served as the calling station's own claimant
operations. What stays forbidden is the adapter keeping anything alive on a
station's behalf.

Open: whether a station's Keelson liveliness token may count as its heartbeat.
Leaning no (it survives a hung application); settle with Keelson's conn
interface. The `safety_state` stop concern was kept out of the #399 comment
until the stop-sources decision is made.
